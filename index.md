# AlertCoin Native Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Effective date: September 13, 2026

## 1. Service and operator

VibeForge (Data Protection Officer: Seyun Ahn, referred to below as the “Operator”) processes information needed to provide cryptocurrency price information and target-price alerts.

AlertCoin Native has no sign-up or login feature and does not ask you to enter your name, phone number, email address, financial account, transaction history, or payment information. However, identifiers generated by the app and server may be linked to a particular device even though they do not directly identify you by name.

## 2. Information processed and purposes

| Information processed | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Device/user identifiers generated by the app and server, device-linking information, push notification identifiers | Device registration and authentication, distinguishing alerts for each device, and delivering push notifications |

| Exchange, cryptocurrency, target price, alert conditions, enabled status, sound/vibration settings | Creating price alerts, evaluating conditions, and displaying alerts |

| Approximate location inferred from IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostics, advertising and device identifiers | Ad delivery, measurement and analysis, and fraud prevention |
| When analytics sharing is enabled: per-installation analytics identifiers, app/device information, automatic events such as app launches, screen views and engagement time, and approximate location inferred from IP address | Analysing user numbers and app usage and improving the service |

### App usage analytics

Versions offering analytics sharing collect basic usage analytics through Google Analytics for Firebase, without a separate analytics consent pop-up at startup. Analytics sharing is enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. A previous choice to disable analytics sharing is retained. Google LLC may process per-installation analytics identifiers, app version/device/operating system information, app launches, basic screen views, engagement time, automatic events associated with notifications and ads, and approximate location inferred from IP address. This is optional processing to understand user numbers and usage and improve the app.

When you use the app with analytics sharing enabled, information is transmitted over encrypted connections to Google servers and may be processed worldwide, including in the United States. Processing is not limited to a particular country. Detailed-data retention follows section 5 below. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [Analytics data protection information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en).

Analytics advertising ID collection, ad personalization, and optional account data sharing are disabled. The Operator does not send alert target prices, search terms, push tokens, or authentication tokens as Analytics user identifiers or custom information. These settings do not stop all processing by the separate AdMob advertising service.

### Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to provide ads. AdMob processing follows the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

### External APIs for current prices

When you look up a current price, the selected market information and the IP address needed for the network connection are sent directly over an encrypted connection to the Upbit or Bithumb API. App device identifiers and push notification identifiers are not sent. Exact retention periods follow each exchange's policy.

## 4. Required processing and optional features

Device registration, device-linking information, price-alert settings, and push notification identifiers are needed to provide price alerts. Without notification permission, you cannot receive system notifications, but some screens remain usable.

The app currently includes ads, so information related to ad delivery may be processed automatically.

**You can disable app usage analytics sharing in Settings.** Price lookup and alerts remain available. Turning off **App usage analytics sharing** stops new Analytics collection. Records already transmitted are not immediately deleted.

## 5. Retention and deletion

| Data | Retention/deletion rules |
| --- | --- |
| Detailed Analytics user/event data | Retained in Google Analytics for 14 months. New activity restarts the user identifier's retention period. This limit does not apply to standard aggregated reports. |
| Alert history and delivery records | Deleted progressively after 90 days from creation. |
| Device/alert information deleted by the user | Deleted progressively after 30 days from the deletion action, if no need remains to protect other records. |
| Long-unused devices and linked alerts | Deletion begins after 180 days from the last registration/update. |
| Invalid or outdated push notification identifiers | Removed progressively when confirmed invalid. |
| Cryptocurrency search terms | Used only during search requests and not stored in the cryptocurrency database. Search terms that may appear in operational logs are masked. |
| Service request/error logs | Retained for up to 30 days. |
| Backups that may contain server settings and operational logs | Retained for up to 28 days. The operational database is not included in these backups. |

Expired electronic records are securely deleted so that they cannot be recovered, or automatically deleted upon expiry. Deletion may be delayed to the extent necessary to protect related records or meet legal retention requirements.

Copies maintained by external providers for service operation are subject to their contracts and policies. Deleting information in the app does not guarantee immediate deletion of all external copies.

## 6. Resetting notification connections and uninstalling

**Reset notifications and connections** in Settings disables the current device's server alerts, resets push notification identifiers and device links, and registers the same device again. It is not an account deletion feature and does not immediately and completely delete past alert history, operational records, or information processed by external services.

Uninstalling removes locally stored app data according to Android's uninstall procedure. Information already sent to servers or external services follows the retention/deletion rules above.

Resetting notifications and connections does not disable Analytics sharing or delete Google Analytics records. Analytics sharing can be disabled separately in the setting described above.

## 7. Your rights and requests

You or your legal representative may request access, correction/deletion, or suspension of processing. After receiving your email, the Operator will verify your identity or authority to act and whether the requested device and records can be linked to you, then reply with the outcome or reasons for any limitations. We may ask for the minimum additional information necessary for verification.

AlertCoin Native has no user account or email-based identity verification. Some requests may be difficult to fulfil if we cannot reliably establish the relationship between the requester and a particular device or server record. Requests may also be limited by legal retention requirements or the rights of others. We will explain such limitations.

- Email: vibeforgesoft@gmail.com

## 8. Security measures

The Operator applies necessary safeguards, including encryption in transit, protection of device-linking information, restricted access, minimisation of sensitive information in operational logs, and regular deletion. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children

AlertCoin Native is offered on Google Play to people aged 18 or older and does not ask for names or dates of birth. If the target audience changes, safeguards required by applicable law and Google Play policy will be implemented first.

## ONE TAKE — pre-launch privacy notice

Published: October 18, 2026. App: **ONE TAKE** (`com.vibeforge.onetake`).
ONE TAKE is preparing for launch. This advance notice describes the features currently prepared and their data-processing boundaries.
**Live service-notice push messaging (FCM) remains unconnected and blocked.**
Operational conditions, including retention for analytics and notices, will be finalised and this section updated before launch and actual service operation. This notice does not announce the app's launch or the availability of all optional features.

### ONE TAKE service and on-device data

ONE TAKE is an Android app for recording video, editing production information and effects, and saving/sharing finished videos. It has no sign-up/login, advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Payments for paid downloads are handled by Google Play.

| Data | Purpose and processing location | Retention/deletion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera video, microphone audio, recordings, and finished videos | Recording, editing, playback, and saving on the device | Subject to the app's recording review and media deletion procedures. Videos in shared media storage may remain after uninstalling. |
| Brand/production titles, participant information, and editing state | Creation and recovery in app-private storage on the device | Editing state and settings are excluded from automatic backup. They may be lost on uninstall; recovery after reinstalling is not guaranteed. |
| Listing information, thumbnails, temporary processing data, and settings | On-device listing, editing, and playback | Subject to feature-specific cleanup and Android storage lifecycles. Copies made by the user must be managed separately. |

Production information may include names entered by the user. Videos, audio, thumbnails, and the original text of production information are not uploaded as ONE TAKE usage analytics or service-notice data. If you select another app through sharing, that app receives read access to the selected finished video. Subsequent processing follows the receiving service's policy.

Camera and microphone permissions are needed to record video and audio. Denying them limits recording; you can change permissions in Android Settings. Uninstalling the app, deleting videos from shared storage, and deleting data sent to external services are separate actions.

### Optional ONE TAKE usage analytics

Analytics sharing is configured to be **OFF by default and collected only after explicit consent**. Recording, editing, saving, and sharing remain available if you do not consent or later turn it off. AlertCoin's default-ON setting does not apply.

The app analytics prepared for transmission to Google Analytics for Firebase after consent comprise screen categories, successful production steps, save results, and predefined error categories. Events do not include video, original production text, media locations, production IDs, push tokens, or app-assigned user IDs. The SDK may process per-installation analytics identifiers, app/operating system/device/language information, automatic analytics such as app launches and engagement time, and approximate location based on IP address. Advertising ID collection, ad personalization, and notice-linked Analytics reporting are configured to be disabled.

Turning analytics off blocks new app-event transmission and requests that SDK collection and storage stop. App events are not accumulated while OFF for later transmission. Records already in transit or received by servers are not necessarily cancelled or deleted immediately.

**To be finalised before operation:** the actual scope of SDK automatic collection, detailed-data retention and reset-on-activity settings, aggregated analytics, optional data sharing, and external integrations. Another app's 14-month retention period is not applied to ONE TAKE. This notice must not be used as the final launch Data safety disclosure before those matters are settled.

### Optional ONE TAKE updates and service notices

**Live FCM connectivity is currently blocked. The following describes processing if this feature is offered in the future.** Notice consent is separate from analytics consent and is OFF by default. Both explicit in-app consent and Android notification/channel permission are required. Declining does not prevent video creation. Advertising, promotional, or personalised messages will not be sent.

When connected, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations may use installation identifiers, registration information, app version, SDK/device information, and information needed for communication to deliver notices. The Operator will not maintain a separate token database. Notices will not contain personal video or production information.

Turning notices off first blocks local display, opening, and pending actions. Unsubscription and registration deletion may complete later, depending on connectivity and SDK processing. Communication or temporary registration may occur to clean up earlier operations. Completion deadlines are not guaranteed after uninstalling, prolonged inactivity, or while offline. Registration deletion does not mean deletion of the installation ID, Analytics data, or all server records.

The app is prepared to retain only environment/notice IDs and expiry times for deduplication, up to 128 entries on the device. Notices have a maximum validity of 24 hours, and expired entries are cleaned up during the next processing operation. This delivery validity period is not the retention period for all Google records. Notice bodies and tokens are not kept in a permanent app archive.

### ONE TAKE external processing, enquiries, and changes

When optional features operate, data sent to Google uses the relevant service's encrypted communications and may be processed on Google infrastructure outside Korea. Processing and storage exclusively in Korea are not promised. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en), [Analytics data protection information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en), and [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

AlertCoin's Supabase/Oracle servers, price-alert retention/backup periods, and legal basis for international processing do not apply to ONE TAKE. **Before operating ONE TAKE's optional features, we will finalise and disclose recipients, processing roles, countries, purposes, timing/methods, retention periods, legal grounds, and how to refuse processing and the effects of refusal.** Data and copies already processed by external services follow those services' retention/deletion procedures; uninstalling does not immediately remove everything.

For enquiries and requests for access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing, contact **vibeforgesoft@gmail.com**. As there are no accounts, it may be difficult to establish the relationship between a requester and particular SDK records. We will explain the outcome or limitations. Turning analytics OFF or deleting an FCM registration will not be presented as confirmation that all historical records have been deleted. Specific retention periods for enquiry emails and handling records, and verification/reply procedures, will be finalised and added here before launch.

The target audience age and Google Play content rating will be determined before launch. AlertCoin's age-18-and-over criterion does not automatically apply. If actual features or processing change, this section, in-app notices, and Google Play Data safety information will be updated together.
